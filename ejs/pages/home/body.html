Welcome to the ART!

From the smallest stars to the largest galaxies, the Astrostatistics Research Team (ART) is an interdisciplinary research group using cutting-edge statistics and artificial intelligence to unravel the mysteries of the Universe! Please follow the links for more information about the exciting work that we do and the wonderful people who make it all happen.

Statstro Workshop

Since 2021, the ART has also organized "Statstro" (originally "Stellar Stats" from 2021-2023), an annual workshop that aims to bring together researchers from astrophysics, statistics, computer science, and other related fields to celebrate interdisciplinary research, discuss ongoing challenges, and forge new connections. While originally a single day open only to University of Toronto affiliates, the workshop has since broadened to researchers from other institutions and grown into a two-day hybrid event with no registration fee. Each themed session now pairs a keynote overview talk with a hands-on coding tutorial and a contributed science talk, alongside lightning talks and poster sessions, with a particular focus on early-career researchers. More information can be found on the official Statstro website.
